# Winston Academy Coin (WAC)

Winston Academy Coin (WAC) is the learn-to-earn token at the heart of Winston's blockchain education platform on Binance Smart Chain. Rather than charging users for education, WAC rewards you for actively learning and maintaining engagement with blockchain knowledge.

## Contract Details

- **Contract Address:** `0xc01e8687eE397106aCDd52BbCc9f2E1E0cEe2aC1`
- **Chain:** Binance Smart Chain (BSC)
- **Standard:** BEP-20
- **Decimals:** 18

## How to Earn WAC

WAC is earned through participation in Winston's three core educational features:

**Quiz Participation** — Answer `/quiz` questions to build your knowledge and score points that convert to WAC rewards. The harder the questions, the more you earn.

**Asking Questions** — Use `/ask` to get personalized answers on blockchain topics from our AI education system (WiseGuy). Each interaction deepens your understanding.

**Maintaining Streaks** — Build consecutive daily quiz participation streaks for bonus multipliers. The longer your streak, the more WAC you accumulate.

Your quiz profile tracks four metrics: **points**, **streak length**, **rank among learners**, and **accuracy percentage**. All contribute to your WAC earning potential.

## Redeeming WAC

Once you've accumulated WAC, you can redeem it directly from the academy treasury using the `/redeem` command. Redemptions are backed by real tokens held on-chain — no IOUs, no delays.

### The Academy Treasury Model

The academy treasury is automatically funded by **20% of all swap fees** collected on TradeTower. This creates a powerful incentive loop:

1. More traders use TradeTower to swap tokens
2. Each trade incurs a 3% AMM fee
3. 20% of those fees flow directly into the academy treasury
4. A larger treasury means more WAC available for learners
5. More generous rewards attract new students
6. Better-educated traders use TradeTower more actively
7. The cycle accelerates

**The result:** The academy is self-sustaining. As the Winston ecosystem grows, education funding grows automatically—no VC capital needed, no ad revenue, no hidden costs.

## Global Learn-to-Earn Standard

WAC represents a shift in how blockchain education is funded. Instead of the traditional model where students pay tuition, Winston's model inverts the economics: the ecosystem pays students to learn. This approach:

- **Removes barriers to entry** — Free education accessible to anyone
- **Aligns incentives** — Better traders create more swap fees, which funds more learning
- **Builds community** — Everyone benefits from everyone else's success
- **Creates permanence** — Not dependent on investor funding rounds or corporate sponsorship

The long-term vision is for WAC to become the standard for learn-to-earn mechanisms across blockchain education platforms, extending beyond Discord to Telegram, X, and web platforms as the Winston ecosystem matures.

## On-Chain Transparency

All WAC balances, treasury state, and redemption history are publicly verifiable on the BSC blockchain. You can check your balance, the treasury balance, and the transaction history at any time using a BSC block explorer. True decentralized education funding.
