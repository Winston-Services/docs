# Winston (WIN)

## Overview

Winston (WIN) is a deflationary rewards token on Binance Smart Chain. By design, the majority of the supply is permanently locked, creating natural scarcity that benefits remaining holders as the platform grows.

## Deflationary Design

- **51%+ of total supply permanently locked** — Never enters circulation
- **21% sent to burn address** — Removed from supply permanently
- **Remaining supply appreciates** as trading volume increases and transaction fees accumulate
- Holders benefit from transaction fees and platform growth

## Member Rewards

WIN holders earn 26% of all TradeTower swap fee revenue, distributed proportionally based on:
- Your WIN balance on the snapshot date
- 30-day rolling average of holdings
- Non-LP tokens only (liquidity provider tokens excluded)

More trading volume = larger fee distribution pool = higher rewards.

## Use Cases

- **Liquidity Pairs** — WIN/RKL trading pair on TradeTower
- **LP Pools** — Stake liquidity to earn transaction fees
- **Rewards Distribution** — Automatic rewards to all holders
