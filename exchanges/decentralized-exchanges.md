# Exchanges

## TradeTower — The Primary Exchange

**TradeTower is Winston's built-in DEX and the recommended place to trade.** It's an Automated Market Maker (AMM) running on Discord and REST API, using the constant-product model (like Uniswap v2).

### TradeTower Advantages

- **Deepest Winston Ecosystem Liquidity** — All pools are native to Winston's economy, ensuring strong trading pairs and tight spreads
- **3% Fees Fund Community** — Every trade directly supports academy education, node operators, member rewards, and liquidity recycling
- **No External Wallet Needed** — Trade directly from your TradeTower account using Discord commands or REST API
- **Instant Settlements** — All trades settle immediately on the internal ledger

### TradeTower Commands

- `/swap` — Exchange one token for another
- `/quote` — See the price and expected output before trading
- `/pools` — View available trading pairs and liquidity pools
- `/addLiquidity` — Become a liquidity provider and earn swap fees
- `/removeLiquidity` — Exit your LP position and reclaim your tokens

### How Fees Work

Each trade on TradeTower charges a **3% AMM fee**, distributed across the Winston ecosystem:

- **20%** → Winston Academy (funds learn-to-earn rewards)
- **8.4% + 16.8% + 2.8%** → Development, management, and marketing (via governance)
- **26%** → Member rewards (distributed to asset holders)
- **7.8%** → Node operator payouts (network infrastructure)
- **6.5%** → Liquidity recycling (auto-adds back to LP pools)
- **11.7%** → Asset management (via governance)

Unlike centralized exchanges, these fees don't disappear—they fund the entire Winston economy.

## Which Exchange Should I Use?

**For most users: TradeTower.** It has:

- The most Winston ecosystem tokens available
- The tightest spreads (deepest native liquidity)
- Direct fee benefits (your fees fund the ecosystem you're part of)
- No need to bridge or manage external wallets

**Use external DEXes if:**

- You want to trade on a specific blockchain for other reasons
- You're comparing prices across multiple venues (arbitrage)
- You're trading pairs not available on TradeTower
- You prefer a particular DEX interface

## Gas and Transaction Costs

**TradeTower:** No gas fees (trades settle on the internal ledger using Discord or REST API).

**External DEXes:** Standard blockchain gas fees apply depending on the chain (ETH, BNB, POL, ARB, etc.). Check the chain's current gas prices before trading.

## Liquidity Providers

If you want to earn fees by providing liquidity, **TradeTower is the place.** Use `/addLiquidity` to become an LP and earn a share of all swap fees for your pools. External DEXes offer LP opportunities too, but TradeTower rewards flow directly to your account with zero complexity.
