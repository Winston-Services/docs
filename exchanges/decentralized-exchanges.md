### Decentralized Exchange Platforms

Decentralized exchanges (DEXs) offer a variety of platforms for trading, including:

* **SushiSwap**: Available on multiple networks such as Harmony, Gnosis, Arbitrum, and Polygon.
* **PancakeSwap**: Popular on the Binance Smart Chain for token swaps.
* **QuickSwap and SMBSwap**: Known for their transaction efficiency on the Polygon network.
* **ApeSwap**: Supports multiple blockchains including Binance Smart Chain.
* **HoneySwap**: Known for low-cost trading on alternative networks.
* **Uniswap**: Dominant on the Ethereum network for decentralized finance (DeFi) operations.
