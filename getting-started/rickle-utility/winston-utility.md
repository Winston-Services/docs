# Winston Utility

The Winston total circulating supply continuously diminishes as all transaction volumes contribute to the permanently locked contract.



How? A significant portion of the Winston token over time has been sent to and locked directly in the Winston Contract FOREVER (this is in addition to the typical 0x000...dEaD address). The initial paired LP was also sent to and locked into this Winston Contract Address forever. As volume occurs, a very small trading fee is earned by the LP holder... the Winston Contract Address, sending more Winston to the forever locked address with each and every transaction. This includes MEV and arbitrage bot volume as well!
