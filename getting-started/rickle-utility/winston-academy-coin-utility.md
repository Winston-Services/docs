# Winston Academy Coin Utility

The Winston Academy Coin is a specialized token designed to facilitate and incentivize educational interactions on our platform. It is used to compensate both educators and students who contribute to the learning process, thereby enhancing the overall educational experience. Revenue generated from our project is strategically allocated to bolster the liquidity of the Academy Coin, thereby underpinning its value.

At present, the Winston Academy Coin is not publicly available. It will be exclusively integrated within our forthcoming online academy, which is dedicated to blockchain technology and the effective utilization of its associated technology stack. This targeted approach ensures that the token directly supports and enriches the educational framework of the academy.
