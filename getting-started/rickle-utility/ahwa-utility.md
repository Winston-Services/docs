# Ahwa Utility

Ahwa functions as the governance token within our ecosystem, empowering holders to actively influence the project's leadership and strategic direction. As an Ahwa voter, you gain the ability to submit proposals and vote on critical decisions that shape the future of our platform, including its operational and developmental priorities.

In terms of liquidity management, Ahwa is supported by traditional liquidity pools that include stable tokens and BNB, which help maintain its stability and liquidity. Additionally, Ahwa is strategically paired with Winston, establishing a vital link between these two tokens. This pairing not only facilitates arbitrage opportunities but also enhances the overall value and liquidity of both tokens.

Through these arbitrage activities and the dynamic interactions between Ahwa and Winston, additional volume is generated, which further contributes to the permanently locked Winston token. This synergy drives increased activity and liquidity across various pools, thereby strengthening the ecosystem and reinforcing the value of both tokens.
