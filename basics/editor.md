# Adding Tokens in MetaMask

**1. Open MetaMask**

* Launch your MetaMask browser extension or mobile app.
* Unlock your wallet with your password if needed.

**2. Switch to the Binance Smart Chain Network**

* Ensure you are on the **Binance Smart Chain (BSC)** network by selecting it from the network dropdown.

**3. Go to the “Assets” Tab**

* Navigate to the **Assets** tab to view the tokens currently in your wallet.

**4. Click on “Import Tokens”**

* Scroll to the bottom of your asset list and click the **Import Tokens** button.

**5. Add Custom Tokens**

For tokens not automatically listed, follow these steps to add them manually:

* Click on the **Custom Token** tab.
*   For each token, paste the corresponding contract address below:

    **Tokens to Add (BSC):**

    * **Winston**: 0x75578ebBefe274F240B8E1b5859cA34f342157D9
    * **Rickle**: 0xeCa15e1BbFF172D545Dd6325F3Bae7b737906737
    * **Ahwa**: 0x3A81caafeeDCF2D743Be893858cDa5AcDBF88c11
* The Token Symbol and Decimals (18) should auto-populate. Click **Next**, then **Add Tokens**.

**6. View and Manage Tokens**

* After adding, your tokens will appear in the **Assets** tab, ready for use.

By following these simple steps, you can add custom tokens like Winston, Rickle, and Ahwa to your MetaMask wallet on Binance Smart Chain.
