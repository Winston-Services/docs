# Adding Tokens in Trust Wallet

**1. Open Trust Wallet**

* Launch the **Trust Wallet** app on your mobile device.

**2. Ensure You Are on Binance Smart Chain**

* Before proceeding, make sure you are interacting with tokens on the **Binance Smart Chain (BSC)**. Your wallet should automatically support BSC, but confirm this by selecting **BSC** for transactions related to these tokens.

**3. Go to the Tokens Tab**

* From the main screen, click the **Tokens** tab at the bottom to view your assets.

**4. Add Custom Tokens**

* In the **Tokens** tab, click the **+** icon in the top-right corner to add a custom token.
* Switch the network to **Binance Smart Chain (BEP-20)** by clicking on **Ethereum** and selecting **Smart Chain** from the dropdown.

**5. Enter Token Details**

For each token, enter the following contract addresses and ensure the token details like symbol and decimals populate automatically:

**Tokens to Add (BSC):**

* **Winston**: 0x75578ebBefe274F240B8E1b5859cA34f342157D9
* **Rickle**: 0xeCa15e1BbFF172D545Dd6325F3Bae7b737906737
* **Ahwa**: 0x3A81caafeeDCF2D743Be893858cDa5AcDBF88c11
* If the token information doesn’t populate automatically, manually enter the token symbol and decimal (usually 18).
* Click **Done** to save each token.

**6. View and Manage Tokens**

* Once added, the tokens will appear in your main wallet screen under **Tokens**.

By following these steps, you can easily add custom tokens like Winston, Rickle, and Ahwa to your Trust Wallet on the Binance Smart Chain network.
